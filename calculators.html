Why this matters
Life insurance pays when you die. Critical illness cover pays when you survive — heart attack, cancer, stroke. Your employer medical covers hospital bills. It does NOT cover lost income during recovery, which can last 12–24 months.
Key question for client
"If you were diagnosed with cancer tomorrow and could not work for 18 months — your employer medical covers your treatment. But who covers your salary? Your mortgage? Your children's school fees? How long could your savings last?"

Disability & Income Protection
Pending ▼
The most overlooked gap for UAE expats
UAE has no state disability benefits for expats. No DEWS payout for disability. If you cannot work permanently — your salary stops, your visa is cancelled, and you must leave the country. Without a plan, your family has nothing.
Key question for client
"If you were in an accident tomorrow and could never work again — what income would your family have? In the UK there is state support. In India your family would help. In UAE — there is only what you planned for. What have you planned?"
